Privacy Policy — Lexithra

Last updated: 3 June 2026

Developer: Vasil Shelelo · Contact: vvddnn0109@gmail.com

1. Information we collect

The App itself does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email, or contact details.

The App stores game progress locally on your device (best score, gems, settings, daily missions). This data never leaves your device unless your device's own backup (e.g. Google backup) is enabled by you.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect and process certain data automatically, such as device identifiers (advertising ID), IP address and approximate location, and app/ad interaction data.

This data is processed by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

4. Purchases

The App does not currently offer in-app purchases.

5. Your rights

Depending on your region you may have rights to access, correct, or delete data. For ad-related data, manage it via your device settings (reset/limit advertising ID) and the in-app consent form. For other requests, contact vvddnn0109@gmail.com.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy: Vasil Shelelo, vvddnn0109@gmail.com.
