Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 June 2026

Developer: Vasil Shelelo

Contact: vvddnn0109@gmail.com

1. Information we collect

The App itself does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email, or contact details.

The App stores game progress locally on your device (best score, gems, settings, daily missions). This data never leaves your device unless your device's own backup (e.g. Google backup) is enabled by you.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and process certain data automatically, such as:

  • Device identifiers (e.g. advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate (non-precise) location derived from it
  • App and ad interaction data

This data is processed by Google in accordance with:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

Consent (EEA/UK/Switzerland)

For users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the App shows a consent form (Google UMP) on first launch, allowing you to accept or decline personalized advertising. You can change your choice later in the App's settings.

3. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the relevant age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Ads are configured for a general audience.

4. Purchases

The App does not currently offer in-app purchases.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data collected for ads is shared only with Google and its advertising partners as described above, solely to provide advertising services.

6. Your rights

Depending on your region you may have rights to access, correct, or delete data, and to withdraw advertising consent. For ad-related data, manage it via your device settings (reset/limit advertising ID) and the in-app consent form. For other requests, contact us at vvddnn0109@gmail.com.

7. Data retention

Local game data remains until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. Ad-related data is retained by Google per its own policies.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date will change accordingly. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out to Vasil Shelelo at vvddnn0109@gmail.com.
