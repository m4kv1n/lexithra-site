How to Play

Three steps to word puzzle mastery

1

Drag Letters

Pick a letter from your tray and drag it onto the 8x8 board. Smart previews show you where valid words can be formed.

2

Build Words

Form words horizontally or vertically using at least 3 letters. Longer words earn more points!

3

Clear & Combo

Cleared words explode off the board. Chain multiple words in one move for massive combo multipliers.
